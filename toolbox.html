The Toolbox

Sixteen ready-to-use templates, protocols, forms and posters covering all seven guidelines. Every one is free to print, adapt and adopt. Text in [square brackets] is meant to be replaced with your own details.

Each template opens as its own page with a print button — use it to save a PDF, or copy the text into your own document system. Review anything you adopt against your state's legal requirements and your organization's insurance and governance obligations.

Adoption

Making these your own

None of these templates work as drop-in policy without adaptation. They are written to be edited — the bracketed choices exist because the right answer genuinely differs between a volunteer-run recreation league and a club with paid staff and a board.

A sensible order

  1. Code of Conduct and the Safeguarding Audit — these two establish your baseline and tell you what else you need.
  2. Mental Health Point Person role description — naming an owner unlocks most of the rest.
  3. Get Help widget and Bathroom Stall Poster — the fastest visible change, achievable in an afternoon.
  4. Developmental Charter and Commitment Pledge — publish before the next registration cycle opens.
  5. Triage vs. Therapy — put these into your next staff meeting.
  6. The reflection, survey and re-entry tools as your season calendar reaches them.

Before you adopt anything

  • Check your state's mandated reporting law — it governs your reporting policy, not this template.
  • Check your insurer's requirements; some carriers specify screening and supervision standards.
  • If you operate under a national governing body, their policy takes precedence where it is stricter.
  • Have someone with legal training review anything that carries a signature.
  • Records containing health information may be subject to privacy law — store them accordingly.

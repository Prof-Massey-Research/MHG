Where the law stands

Policy Tracker

Physical safety in youth sport is regulated nationwide — every state requires concussion protocols. Mental health is not. This living map tracks the state laws and bills that begin to close that gap.

Why a tracker

A patchwork is forming — fast

Forty-five states have laws on the books covering safeguarding, abuse reporting or coach training. Four require coaches to be trained in mental health. Ohio led the way; Maryland was the first to extend the requirement to public universities. Without a shared national standard, a young athlete's protection depends on their ZIP code.

Tracking this momentum — and linking directly to the bills — helps advocates, leagues and lawmakers learn from one another, and move from a patchwork toward a floor of care every young athlete can count on.

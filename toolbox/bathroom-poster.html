Appendix 5.6 · Print-ready poster

Bathroom Stall Poster

A discreet poster for locker rooms and restroom stalls — the one place a young person can read help information without being watched.

Supports Guideline 06 — Locating Resources & Services

Read the guideline

Why the bathroom stall

Restroom stalls and locker rooms are among the few places a young person can read help information completely unobserved. Print this, fill in your own contact details, and post it at eye level in every stall. Replace it when it gets worn — a faded poster reads as a program that stopped caring.

Your mental health matters as much as your game.

You don't have to face this alone.

Free, confidential help is available 24/7.

Customising the poster

  • Replace [Your Mental Health Point Person] with a real name and number. A national line plus a local human is far more effective than national lines alone.
  • Add a QR code linking to your full resource directory. Free QR generators are widely available — point it at the page on your own website, not a third-party link that may expire.
  • Produce it in the primary languages spoken by your families.
  • Post in every restroom stall, locker room and any private space athletes use.
  • Review the numbers annually as part of your resource directory update.

Printing notes

Use Print or save as PDF at the top of this page, set paper to US Letter or A4 portrait, and enable background graphics if your browser offers it. Laminating or using a plastic sleeve makes a real difference in a humid locker room.

Using this template

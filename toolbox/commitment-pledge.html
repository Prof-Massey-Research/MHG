Developmental Commitment Pledge

A short signed agreement setting shared expectations between the organization, coaches and parents/guardians at registration.

Supports Guideline 02 — Long-Term Athlete Development

Read the guideline

Our shared expectations

For our organization

We will continuously evaluate our coaches based on their adherence to these developmental standards, providing them with the education and tools they need to be effective teachers.

For our coaches

You are educators first. You will plan engaging, age-appropriate practices, communicate transparently with families, and reward the process of learning rather than punishing mistakes.

For our parents and guardians

You are our developmental partners. We ask that you support the learning process, reframe success around effort and enjoyment, and help us monitor your child's physical and emotional well-being throughout the season.

Our program's developmental pledge

Our organization follows a Long-Term Athlete Development (LTAD) model. This means we prioritize your child's long-term physical, mental and emotional growth over short-term wins and weekend trophies. By joining this program, you agree to the following core principles.

Development over scoreboards

At the early stages, field time, playing positions and coaching attention are distributed to develop all players — not just the early bloomers.

Multi-sport athletes welcome

We encourage our athletes to play other sports. We will not punish players for missing off-season or optional training to participate in another sport.

Biological pacing

We restrict practice hours, limit travel and mandate rest days based on medical and developmental science, to prevent burnout and overuse injuries.

Partners in the process

Parents and guardians are our partners. We ask that you praise your child's effort, resilience and skill acquisition rather than the final score or their stats.

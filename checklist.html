Before you start

This is a planning tool, not a certification or accreditation. Its purpose is to make your strengths visible and your remaining work finite.

Answer honestly rather than aspirationally — an item counts only if it is actually happening now, not if it is planned. Most programs find they are already doing more than they expected in some guidelines and less in others. That is the point.

After the checklist

Turning gaps into a plan

1. Reach Foundational everywhere first

Any guideline not yet at Foundational is the priority, regardless of how advanced the others are. Breadth before depth.

2. Assign every open item an owner and a date

The downloaded report lists outstanding items by guideline. Put a name and a target date against each one before the next season starts.

3. Re-run this annually

Drift is the default. Repeating the assessment each year — alongside the safeguarding audit — is what keeps the work from quietly unwinding.

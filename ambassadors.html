Voices that move kids

Athlete Ambassadors

Champions who know the pressures of elite sport — and use their platforms to make it safe for the next generation to speak up.

  • MF

    Missy Franklin

    Five-time Olympic gold medallist · Swimming

    One of the most decorated swimmers in U.S. history, Missy Franklin Johnson has been candid about the mental health challenges that accompany elite competition. She served on the expert panel that shaped these guidelines, and champions a youth sport culture where seeking help is seen as a strength.

  • MB

    Montee Ball

    Former NFL and collegiate running back

    A record-setting college running back and former NFL player, Montee Ball speaks openly about his struggles with mental health and his recovery. Today he is a sought-after advocate and speaker, helping coaches and young athletes understand that asking for help early can change the course of a life.

When the adults in sport make it normal to talk about mental health, kids listen. That is where culture change begins.

More ambassadors coming

We are building a roster of athletes across sports and levels. If you would like to join as an ambassador, or know someone who should, we would like to hear from you.
